# Mercenary Operations

The persistent mercenary organization and the forces it commands in the BattleTech setting.

## Language

**Mercenary force**:
The persistent player-controlled organization, which continues through personnel deaths and departures.

**Lance**:
A formation of four BattleMechs.

**Company**:
A formation of three lances, used as a rough scale for the game rather than a strict roster or deployment limit.

**Roster**:
The BattleMechs owned by the mercenary force, distinct from those committed to a particular mission.

**MechWarrior roster**:
The mercenary force’s personnel, including healthy warriors, injured warriors, and deceased warriors retained in memoriam.

**Pilot assignment**:
The persistent pairing of one living MechWarrior with one owned BattleMech, independent of deployment.

**Storage**:
Spare weapons, ammunition, heat sinks and jump jets held by the mercenary force and not installed on any BattleMech. Armor and fixed systems are never stored.

**Market**:
Equipment for sale where the DropShip is in orbit, bought from the hangar's Market tab. Bought items are installed directly on a BattleMech as pending purchases.

**Pending purchase**:
A market item installed in a pending loadout. It is paid for when that loadout is applied and cancelled, at no cost, if the item is taken out, the loadout is reset, or the changes are abandoned.

**Critical slot**:
One of the fixed equipment spaces in a BattleMech location: six in the head and each leg, twelve in each torso and arm.

**Loadout**:
The equipment installed in a particular owned BattleMech: its stock design less any weapons, ammunition, heat sinks and jump jets removed in the hangar, plus any spares installed there from storage.

**Pending changes**:
Loadout changes, including pending purchases, made in the hangar but not yet applied to the BattleMech. They are kept per BattleMech until applied, reset, or abandoned on leaving the hangar.

**Ready MechWarrior**:
A healthy MechWarrior available for immediate deployment, whether currently assigned to a BattleMech or unassigned.

**Deployment**:
The force committed to a particular mission.

**MechWarrior**:
A pilot who operates a BattleMech.
_Avoid_: Mech pilot

**Contract**:
An agreement committing the mercenary force to work for an employer; it may encompass multiple missions.

**Mission**:
An individual operational assignment within a contract.

**Mission offer**:
A single-mission contract on the mission offer board, with fixed terms: location, exact expected opposition, deadline, payment, salvage picks and an insurance limit.

**Mission offer board**:
The ten mission offers available at a time, oldest first. Each day the oldest may be taken off and the board is refilled.

**Initial draft**:
The selection of four starting BattleMechs and four starting MechWarriors from eight simultaneously visible pools, with three options of similar caliber in each pool.

**Repair order**:
Authorized work to fully repair a particular BattleMech, with a quoted cost and completion date.

**Mission result**:
The battlefield facts reported after a deployment, from which the mercenary force determines contractual and financial consequences.

**Settlement**:
The resolution of a contract's payment and contractual penalties following its outcome.

**Debt**:
An unpaid financial obligation of the mercenary force carried from a missed quarterly payment, due alongside the next quarterly payment.

**Bankruptcy**:
The forced dissolution of the mercenary force when, at the end of its three-month grace period, it cannot pay both carried debt and the new quarterly payment.

**Wreck**:
A destroyed BattleMech recovered from a mission, distinct from an unrecoverable mech, which may retain sale value.

**Attack order**:
A command to reach and attack a specified target, with the MechWarrior finding a path as necessary.

**Priority target**:
A preferred target for a MechWarrior to select when an opportunity presents, without itself directing movement or pursuit.
_Avoid_: Attack order

**Salvo**:
The missiles an LRM or SRM rack fires in one discharge, each rolling its own hit and location.

**Burst**:
The one-second stream of tracers a machine gun fires in one discharge, resolved by a single hit roll.
_Avoid_: Volley, which is a MechWarrior's selection of weapons to fire together

**Indirect fire**:
An LRM salvo at a priority or Attack target the shooter cannot see, guided by a spotter.

**Spotter**:
An active friendly BattleMech with its own clear sight to the target of an indirect salvo.

**Electro-interference**:
The temporary accuracy penalty a PPC hit imposes on every attack its target makes.

**Preferred engagement distance**:
The player-adjustable distance a MechWarrior attempts to establish and maintain from the target of an attack order.

**Last-known position**:
The position at which an enemy was last detected, which does not track its movement after detection is lost.

**Simulated deployment**:
A practice battle launched from the mech hangar that reports a combat outcome without changing the mercenary force or campaign.

**Force selection**:
The choice of BattleMechs and assigned MechWarriors to participate in a deployment, including a simulated deployment.

**Disabled BattleMech**:
A BattleMech unable to continue fighting because it has lost both legs or all usable weapons, distinct from a destroyed BattleMech.

**Destroyed BattleMech**:
A BattleMech whose head or center torso has been destroyed, distinct from a disabled BattleMech. If recovered from a mission, it is a wreck.

**Hardpoint**:
A weapon mount slot on a chassis model, named after its MW5 socket (`Forearm_Right_BH1`): ballistic, energy, missile or AMS, in one damage location.

**Weapon visual**:
One mesh a hardpoint can show, such as `Laser`, `AC10`, `Missile6`, or a `Blank` cover for an emptied mount.

**Mount map**:
The hardpoint → weapon visual assignment a mech model is built from, resolved from its weapons.

**Biome**:
The kind of world a battlefield lies on (Valley, Arctic, Lunar, Highlands, Desert, Tropical, Grassland, Archipelago, Volcanic or Toxic), setting its climate, ground shape, features and look.

**Ambient temperature**:
A biome's temperature on the mech heat scale. Mech heat drifts toward it: hotter mechs gain free cooling, colder ones warm up.

**Cooling efficiency**:
A biome's multiplier on heat-sink output. It does not affect ambient exchange.

**Settlement**:
A cluster of solid buildings on a levelled pad of the battlefield.
