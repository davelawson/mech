# Resolve combat continuously with independent weapon cooldowns

Tactical combat uses continuous movement and independent weapon cooldowns rather than shared tabletop combat rounds. BattleTech-inspired hit and location-damage resolution provide the first slice’s rules foundation, with heat and criticals deferred, while continuous timing makes weapon readiness and firing rate part of tactical decisions from the outset. This prioritizes real-time battlefield behavior over preserving tabletop turn structure; exact timing values remain provisional.
